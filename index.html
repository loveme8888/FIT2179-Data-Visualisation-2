MALAYSIA TOURISM

Insights. Trends. Growth.

Malaysia's tourism performance strengthened in 2024, driven by stronger regional connectivity, rising visitor expenditure, and concentrated destination demand across key states.

Tourism Market Patterns

Which source markets drove arrivals, and which markets created higher receipt value.

Market Lens

Arrival volume identifies the largest source markets, while receipts show how market value differs from market size.

Main Pattern

Singapore remained the largest source market, while China stood out as a high-value market in visitor receipts.

Why It Matters

Market strategy depends on both visitor numbers and spending value, so high-arrival and high-receipt markets should be read together.

Tourism Destination Patterns

Which states concentrated international hotel guests and domestic visitors.

Visitor Spending & Domestic Behaviour

How international visitors spent money, and how domestic tourists travelled within Malaysia.

Behaviour Lens

Spending and domestic travel patterns reveal what visitors did after arrival, not just where demand came from.

Main Pattern

Shopping was the largest international spending category, while domestic tourism was shaped by social trips, road movement, and informal stays.

Why It Matters

Combining value and behaviour shows how tourism money enters the economy and how Malaysians move between states.

5

Tourism Opportunity Insights

Where state-level volume, growth, and domestic destination appeal point to stronger tourism opportunities.

Opportunity Lens

Volume shows established demand, while growth highlights where visitor momentum is strengthening most quickly.

Main Pattern

Kelantan, Putrajaya, and Sabah recorded the highest foreign hotel guest growth rates, while domestic destinations show retail, leisure, and nature appeal.

Why It Matters

The strongest opportunity signal is not simply the largest visitor base, but the combination of scale, momentum, and destination draw.

The Big Picture

  • Tourism activity strengthened across international and domestic indicators.
  • Singapore remains the dominant source market.
  • Shopping anchors international visitor value, while domestic travel remains socially and road oriented.
  • State opportunities depend on both scale and growth momentum.
