MALAYSIA TOURISM

Insights. Trends. Growth.

Malaysia's tourism performance strengthened in 2024, driven by stronger regional connectivity, rising visitor expenditure, and concentrated destination demand across key states.

1

Tourism Market Patterns

Where inbound demand came from, and where it physically concentrated.

Demand Focus

Inbound tourism is shaped by nearby regional markets and a small group of high-volume destination states.

Main Pattern

Kuala Lumpur, Selangor, Sabah, Penang, and Pahang form the clearest visitor clusters, while Singapore leads all source markets.

Why It Matters

The market is regional, connected, and spatially uneven, so gateway access and state capacity strongly shape visitor concentration.

Tourism Growth Trends

How visitor flows changed through the year, and which entry modes supported movement.

Trend Signal

Monthly arrivals show whether growth was broad-based through the year or concentrated in selected travel periods.

Main Pattern

Every month in 2024 exceeded its 2023 counterpart, and land transport accounted for 66.1% of foreign visitor entries.

Why It Matters

Growth was supported by year-round demand and regional land connectivity, not only by long-haul air traffic.

Tourism Spending Patterns

How visitor expenditure was distributed, and which markets created higher receipt value.

Value Lens

Tourism value is not only about visitor counts; spending categories show what visitors bought and where receipts were created.

Main Pattern

Shopping is the largest expenditure item, followed by accommodation and food. Growth was strongest in fuel and international airfares, while entertainment declined.

Why It Matters

Malaysia's visitor economy combines high-volume regional movement with higher-value spending from selected regional and long-haul markets.

Shopping remains the largest expenditure category, accounting for more than twice the value of accommodation.

4

Tourism Opportunity Insights

Where state-level visitor volume and growth point to stronger tourism momentum.

Opportunity Lens

Volume shows established demand, while growth highlights where visitor momentum is strengthening most quickly.

Main Pattern

Kelantan, Putrajaya, and Sabah recorded the highest growth rates, while Labuan, Johor, and Pahang declined among the tracked states.

Why It Matters

The strongest opportunity signal is not simply the largest visitor base, but the combination of scale and growth momentum.
