MALAYSIA TOURISM 2024

Insights. Trends. Growth.

Malaysia's tourism recovery accelerated in 2024, driven by stronger regional connectivity, rising visitor expenditure, and concentrated destination demand across key states.

Scroll to explore

The Big Picture

  • International arrivals fully recovered in 2024.
  • Singapore remains the dominant source market.
  • Shopping is the largest expenditure category.
  • Sabah recorded the strongest state-level growth among major states.

Malaysia Overview

Where foreign hotel guests stayed, and which markets drove arrivals.

✈︎

Tourism demand in Malaysia is geographically concentrated, with capital-city hotel demand and nearby state activity forming the strongest cluster. At the same time, inbound arrivals are highly regional: Singapore alone contributes far more visitors than any other source market.
