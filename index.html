MALAYSIA TOURISM

Insights. Trends. Growth.

Malaysia's tourism performance strengthened in 2024, driven by stronger regional connectivity, rising visitor expenditure, and concentrated destination demand across key states.

Tourism Market Patterns

Which source markets generated the largest visitor volumes and tourism value.

Market Lens

Arrival volume identifies the largest source markets, while receipts show how market value differs from market size.

Main Pattern

Singapore remained the largest source market, while China stood out as a high-value market in visitor receipts.

Why It Matters

Market strategy depends on both visitor numbers and spending value, so high-arrival and high-receipt markets should be read together.

Visitor Spending & Behaviour

How visitors spent money, why domestic tourists travelled, and how they moved across Malaysia.

Behaviour Lens

Spending and domestic travel patterns reveal what visitors did after arrival, not just where demand came from.

Main Pattern

Shopping was the largest international spending category, while domestic tourism was shaped by social visits, shopping, and leisure.

Why It Matters

Combining spending, trip purpose, and movement shows how tourism value and travel behaviour spread across the economy.

Source: Malaysia Tourism Statistics in Brief 2024, p. 13.

MY

Shopping anchors international visitor value, while domestic trips are highly social, shopping-led, and connected by state-to-state travel corridors.

The Big Picture

  • Tourism activity strengthened across international and domestic indicators.
  • Singapore remains the dominant source market.
  • Major gateway states still concentrate tourism activity.
  • Shopping anchors international visitor value, while domestic travel remains social and leisure-oriented.
  • Future opportunities depend on both visitor scale and growth momentum.
